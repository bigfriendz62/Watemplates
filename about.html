About WATemplates

WATemplates is a free library of WhatsApp business message templates, built for small business owners who handle customer conversations themselves — often without any dedicated customer service staff.

The idea started from a simple observation: sellers on WhatsApp answer the same handful of questions dozens of times a day — "how much?", "do you deliver?", "is it still available?" — and typing a fresh, professional reply every single time wastes time that could go toward actually growing the business. WATemplates collects proven, ready-to-use replies for the most common situations so sellers can copy, personalize, and send in seconds.

The site is maintained by Goodluck Meshack Akoh, and is free to use with no account or signup required. New templates and categories are added periodically based on real messaging scenarios from small business owners, particularly across Africa, Asia, and the Middle East, where WhatsApp is often the primary sales channel.
