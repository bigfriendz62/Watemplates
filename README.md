# WATemplates — Free WhatsApp Business Message Templates

> 200+ free copy-paste WhatsApp templates for small businesses.

## What it does
- 200+ professional WhatsApp reply templates
- 12 categories: Price Inquiry, Payment, Delivery, Complaints, etc.
- Search functionality
- One-click copy
- SEO optimized for Google search traffic
- Google AdSense ready (add your publisher ID in index.html)
- Links to Replo and Clarity for referral traffic

## How to earn money
1. Sign up for Google AdSense at adsense.google.com
2. Get approved (takes 1-2 weeks)
3. Add your publisher ID to index.html (see comment in file)
4. Earn money from every visitor
